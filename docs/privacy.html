Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Summary

bTab does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data. The extension runs entirely in your browser. There is no backend server, no analytics, no tracking, and no network requests except for license validation (Pro users only).

Data We Don't Collect

  • Your browsing history
  • Tab titles, URLs, or content
  • Personal information
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Cookies or tracking identifiers

Data Stored Locally

bTab stores the following data in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local):

  • Settings: Your preferences (theme, sort order, auto-suspend settings)
  • Workspaces: Saved tab collections (Pro feature)
  • Tab history: Recently closed tabs for Time Machine (Pro feature)
  • License info: Your license key and device ID (Pro users only)

This data never leaves your device and is not synced to any server.

License Validation (Pro Only)

If you purchase a Pro license, the extension communicates with LemonSqueezy's API to:

  • Validate your license key on activation
  • Periodically verify the license is still valid (every 24 hours)

This communication includes only your license key and a device identifier (a hash of your browser's characteristics). No browsing data is ever transmitted.

Permissions Explained

  • tabs: Required to list, switch, close, and manage your tabs
  • activeTab: Required to access the current tab's information
  • storage: Required to save your settings locally
  • alarms: Required for auto-suspend and reminder features
  • sessions: Required to restore recently closed tabs
  • notifications: Required for reminder notifications

Third-Party Services

The only third-party service used is LemonSqueezy for payment processing and license management (Pro users only). Please refer to their privacy policy.

Open Source

bTab is open source. You can review the complete source code at GitHub to verify these privacy claims.

Contact
